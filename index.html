ⓘ Temporary Regression in Destiny Temple

How infusion regression works

In Idle Heroes, players frequently regress high-level infusion heroes temporarily to reclaim materials (Stellars, CoT, Cores of Origin, Sublimation, and Spiritual Essences) to satisfy high Temple milestone conditions without losing original investments.

Refund rates per infusion hero:
• Lv.16–40: 300k Stellars + 300k CoT per level
• Lv.41–75: 300k Stellars + 38k Essence per level
• Lv.41–70: Sublimation branches (up to 180k per branch)
• Lv.56, 61, 66, 71: +1 Core of Origin each (up to 4 Cores at Lv.75)

Any resources recovered are automatically credited toward satisfying your selected Temple target level.

ⓘ Smart Pick Optimizer Logic

Zero-waste allocation algorithm

The optimizer calculates your exact deficits for every resource needed for your chosen target level, then allocates chests in optimal order:

  1. Single-purpose chests (e.g. pure Sublimation) are prioritized to satisfy their specific requirements first.
  2. Flexible chests (e.g. Spheres or Mixed Chests) are dynamically routed to whichever resource has the highest remaining relative deficit.
  3. Once requirements are fully met, surplus chests are tagged as Saved so you don't overspend resources!
